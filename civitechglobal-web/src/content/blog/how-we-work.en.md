Most people approaching a software company carry the same worry: *how do I know I won't be taken for a ride?* It's a fair worry. So here is exactly what working with us looks like — not a slogan, but the path a real project takes.

## The short version: four steps

1. You describe what you need, in your own words. Attach documents if you have them.
2. We read it, and if something is unclear we ask. Asking isn't carelessness — it means we'd rather not guess.
3. You receive a **written proposal**: scope, deliverables, assumptions, what is *not* included, a timeline and a price range.
4. Using your tracking code, you open the proposal and accept or decline it. No chasing, no sales pressure.

## The tracking code — small, but it matters

When you submit a request you get a tracking code. Keep it somewhere safe, because it is your key: whenever you want to see where your work stands, you enter that code.

Why build it this way? Because following your own project shouldn't depend on someone picking up the phone. The [tracking page](/track) is always open — three in the morning included.

## What actually makes us different

Honestly: no company becomes the best by announcing that it is. What we can promise is these four things.

- **Quality.** The code we write is reviewed, tested, and meant to keep working for years — not just on delivery day.
- **Speed.** The path from request to proposal is short and defined. You are not left waiting for someone to "look into it and get back to you".
- **Accessibility.** The site works with a keyboard, works with a screen reader, and opens on an inexpensive phone. That is a decision, not a side feature.
- **Economic sense.** You see a price range before anything starts. If a piece of work isn't worth the money, we say so early.

## If you're not sure yet

You don't have to start with a large project. You can [request a consultation](/consult), tell us a few times that suit you, and we'll call. A consultation commits you to nothing.

## Common questions about how we work

### Do I pay for a quote?

No. Reviewing your request and writing the proposal is free.

### What if I decline the proposal?

Nothing happens. You decline, and that's that. Anything you sent stays with us and goes to no one else.

### I lost my tracking code.

Contact us from the phone number or email address you used when you submitted, and we'll give it to you again.

### Do you take small projects?

Yes. Size isn't how we decide. If something falls outside what we're good at, we'll tell you plainly.
